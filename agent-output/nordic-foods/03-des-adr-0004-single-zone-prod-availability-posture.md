## 🔍 Context

The user accepted single-zone Prod, but the SLA requirement is 99.9%. The approved assessment decides:

> Availability | Single zone, single instance in Prod | User accepted; 99.9% achievability open for sponsor decision.

and warns:

> The 99.9% availability target is not waived by the single-zone preference. Achievability is unverified, so a sponsor decision is open (see Reliability).

Source: [02-architecture-assessment.md](02-architecture-assessment.md), Requirements Validation and Reliability Assessment.

## ✅ Decision

Record the accepted posture only: Prod runs one P0v3 instance in one zone, SQL uses Local redundancy and Blob stays on the user-pinned LRS. This ADR does not close the 99.9% requirement and does not change the approved architecture. Any change below needs Architect review and human approval first.

## 🔄 Alternatives Considered

| Option                                                         | Pros                                           | Cons                                                                                      | WAF Impact                 |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Keep topology, quantify residual risk                          | No added cost                                  | 99.9% not demonstrated; numeric SLAs not yet obtained                                     | Reliability: -             |
| Zone-redundant plan on a repo-listed SKU (P1v3/P2v3 or P1v4/P2v4, two instances) | Raises the App Service SLA | Not priced; P1v3 returned no on-demand meter in `swedencentral`; does not cover SQL or LRS Blob | Reliability: +, Cost: -   |
| P0v3 x2                                                        | Adds capacity and instance redundancy          | $129.94/month; P0v3 is not in the repo matrix, so zone placement is not established        | Performance: +, Cost: -    |
| Revise the 99.9% target                                        | Aligns requirement with design                 | Requires a recorded sponsor decision                                                      | Reliability: →             |

## ⚖️ Consequences

### Positive

- Lowest cost; $348.77/month total is 63% of the EUR 500 budget.

### Negative

- A restart, platform update or zone event interrupts both apps.
- SQL Basic and Standard have no zone redundancy; Premium DTU or vCore would be needed.
- 7-year invoices and receipts sit on LRS-only Blob; a copy or regeneration path is needed.
- No multi-region DR by scope.

### Neutral

- Composite SLA cannot be quoted yet; the serial-path rule still applies.

## 🔒 Compliance Considerations

- No direct GDPR effect; restore must re-apply the deletion ledger (see [ADR-0003](03-des-adr-0003-azure-sql-dtu-entra-only-gdpr-retention.md)).

## 📝 Implementation Notes

- Carry the sponsor decision to Step 3.5 and Step 4; obtain the numeric published SLAs and compute the composite then.
- Run a timed Dev restore at expected data size against the 4-hour RTO and 1-hour RPO.
- Add failed-request and availability alerts on the Prod portal and API.
